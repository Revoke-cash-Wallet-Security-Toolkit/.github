# 🛡️ Revoke.cash Wallet Security Toolkit — Take Back Control of Your Wallet

Welcome to **Revoke.cash Wallet Security Toolkit**, the most widely used tool for managing token approvals and protecting your crypto wallet. Every time you swap, stake, or sell an NFT, you grant a smart contract permission to move your tokens. That access never expires—it sits on your wallet indefinitely, a standing liability if the app is ever exploited. Revoke.cash lets you see every approval you've ever granted and revoke the ones you no longer need.
